Privacy Policy

Hermes Mill · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who uses this app

Hermes Mill is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's Google account is accessed.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets: to read and update spreadsheets in the owner's own account.
  • Google Drive: to find and open files in the owner's own account.
  • Gmail: to search, summarize, label and draft the owner's own email, and to send email only after the owner reviews and approves each message.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used to train general-purpose AI models.
  • Text the owner sends to the assistant may be processed by the AI model provider the owner has chosen, only to produce the answer.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes Mill's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in (consent) screen of this app.
